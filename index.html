Interpretation and limitations

Professional workflow estimates were supplied by a team member and have not been independently validated. Annual volumes, preventable-return rates, effectiveness, checklist duration, wage and setup cost are hypothetical planning inputs. The model does not establish actual statewide savings, approval rates, or authorization delays. The recommended intervention is a measured pilot, not automatic adoption. No patient-level data are used. This static page does not collect or transmit entered values.
