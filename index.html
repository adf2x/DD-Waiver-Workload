This model estimates scenario-level provider time, not approval delays or statewide costs. Correction groups are mutually exclusive and each affected request incurs one correction event.

Interpretation and limitations. Provider task durations came from professional workflow estimates supplied by a project team member and were not independently validated. The number of requests, correction percentages, improved correction rate, and hourly cost are user-selected hypothetical values. The checklist is an illustrative proposed intervention, not a tested reduction. A pend correction may require at least 40 minutes and a substantial rewrite at least 90 minutes; actual time may be greater. Other communications, repeated corrections, Support Coordinator time, and reviewer time are excluded. The model uses full-precision arithmetic internally and rounds displayed hours to one decimal and dollars to the nearest whole dollar. Fractional expected request counts may result from applying rates to totals; these are scenario estimates, not observed people or requests. Annual savings must not be inferred unless the selected request volume represents an annual period. No data is transmitted or stored by this webpage.
